Scope: storefront (home, catalogue, product, quick view, cart sheet, order, sent, saved, contact, 404). Mode: Persuade on home, Operate elsewhere.
Audience: Azerbaijani shoppers on phones; job: find a product, pick a variant, send the order via WhatsApp in under a minute.
Constraints: re-skins per shop via five tokens; AZ/RU/EN; no payment, no delivery; demo data synthetic.

## Direction contract

THESIS: User-pinned: integrate Moxen "Maqueta" — the shop behaves like a native app: objects you pick up, colour that opens around them. The first carpet direction was rejected by the user.
OWN-WORLD: Warm paper, warm ink, coral and blue accents, sand photo grounds; round photos (24/30px), pills, white orbs with warm shadows, glass tab bar, drifting soft shapes and paper grain.
STORY: The visitor taps a product, it lifts into its own coloured screen, they choose, add, and send the order on WhatsApp.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Kicker and big tight headline left, the catalogue button; right a phone mockup with live product cards (phones: a card carousel), shapes behind tinted by the product in focus.
FORM: user-pinned brief (Moxen Maqueta + supplied Counter, Seek, Context meter). seed: none (unavailable). Signature interaction: shared-element flight with edge-drag back.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
